audiogram

Clips from your Spaces, made in the browser

Open the recording of a Space, a podcast or a voice note, drag over the minute worth sharing, and it becomes a video for X: your picture and name, bars that move with the voice, and captions that light up word by word. Tap along to time the captions, pick a shape and colours, and export an MP4. The audio never leaves your computer and there is no watermark. Free and MIT licensed, for what audiogram apps charge a monthly plan for.

npm install github:drkxbt/audiogram
